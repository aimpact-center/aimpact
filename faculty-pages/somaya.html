Deepak Somaya

Dianne and Steven Miller Professor of Business Administration
Faculty Affiliate

Website / dsomaya@illinois.edu

Dr. Deepak Somaya is the Dianne and Steven Miller Professor of Business Administration in the Gies College of Business at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. He also holds appointments in the Illinois College of Law and Discovery Partners Institute. His research interests lie in "big picture" strategic management questions related to knowledge assets (including intellectual property, human capital, and relational capital), digital assets (data, software, AI), and scaling.

Current Research Projects

  • How Data Privacy Affects the Scalability of Digital Firms (with Jingya You). A project that examines the impacts of data privacy on the ability of digital firms to scale, wherein the affects of data privacy (examined using the GDPR shock) on the AI data flywheel is found to significantly reduce the scalability of affected firms.
  • Human-AI Co-Creation (with Babak Hemmatian, Lav Varshney, and other collaborators). A project that uses a controlled interactive test-bed to conduct lab experiments on human-AI co-creation with the goal of examining when and why they produce better creative outcomes, and especially in contrast with human-human collaborations.
